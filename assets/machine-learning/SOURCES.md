# Historical images

- `alan-turing-1951.jpg`: Elliott & Fry, 29 March 1951. Downloaded unchanged from
  https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f8/Alan_Turing_%281951%29.jpg
  Wikimedia Commons identifies this photograph as public domain:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alan_Turing_(1951).jpg
- `turing-paper-excerpt.png`: cropped rendering of the masthead, title, author,
  section heading, and opening question on p. 433 of A. M. Turing,
  *Computing Machinery and Intelligence*, *Mind* 59(236) (1950), 433–460.
  https://doi.org/10.1093/mind/LIX.236.433
  Scan: University of Toronto / JSTOR, journal published by Oxford University Press.
  https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~frank/csc2501/Readings/R1_Turing/Turing-1950.pdf
  Rendered from PDF page index 1 at scale 3 using PDFium, with crop offsets
  `(left=50, bottom=329, right=50, top=32)` PDF points. No full paper is hosted here.

The figure caption links to the source and credits both images. The mathematical
figures are original SVGs generated by `scripts/generate_learning_figures.py`;
they inherit the website's light and dark palettes.
